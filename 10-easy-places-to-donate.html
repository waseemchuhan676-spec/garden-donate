10 Easy Places to Donate Garden Tools Near You

Working links, direct locators, accepted tool lists, and pickup policies for the 10 best organizations to give your garden tools and lawn equipment a second life.

The 10 Best Places to Donate Garden Tools

Click any link or map button below to find the exact donation policy, schedule a pickup, or locate the nearest branch.

🏠

1. Habitat ReStore

Building Material & Tool Resale Nonprofit
🚚 Free Truck Pickup Available 📄 501(c)(3) Tax Receipt

Habitat ReStore locations accept new and gently used tools, lawn mowers, and gardening equipment. Sales proceeds fund affordable housing construction for local families.

✅ Best For:

Riding mowers, push mowers, string trimmers, power tools, wheelbarrows, long-handle shovels, rakes, garden hardware.

🚚 Pickup Rules:

Free truck collection is offered for riding mowers, large garden equipment, or multi-item tool donations. Schedule online 1–2 weeks ahead.

Official ReStore Info → Find Local ReStore on Maps 📍
🤝

2. Goodwill Industries

Charity Thrift & Job Training Centers
📍 Drive-Thru Drop-Off 📄 501(c)(3) Tax Receipt

Goodwill is one of the most accessible drop-off destinations in the nation. Store sales directly fund job training, resume workshops, and employment programs.

✅ Best For:

Clean hand tools (pruners, shears, trowels, cultivators), electric weed eaters, battery tools with chargers, garden pots, hoses.

🚚 Pickup Rules:

Drop-off only at staffed drive-thru donation lanes. Most attendants help unload directly from your trunk.

Official Goodwill Site → Find Nearby Goodwill 📍
❤️

3. The Salvation Army

Charity Thrift & Rehabilitation Services
🚚 Scheduled Home Pickup 📄 501(c)(3) Tax Receipt

Proceeds from Salvation Army Family Stores fund adult rehabilitation centers, homeless shelters, and disaster emergency response.

✅ Best For:

Working gas lawnmowers, edgers, leaf blowers, hedge trimmers, wheelbarrows, garden utility carts, and hand tool sets.

🚚 Pickup Rules:

Go to satruck.org and enter your ZIP code to schedule a free residential pickup truck for large gardening equipment.

Schedule Pickup on satruck.org → Find Drop-Off Location 📍
🌱

4. Community Gardens & Farms

Direct Neighborhood Greening & Food Security
🌿 100% Direct Community Use 📍 Direct Drop-Off

Instead of being resold in a thrift shop, your donated tools are handed directly to neighbors, seniors, and volunteers growing fresh, healthy produce for the community.

✅ Best For:

Long-handled shovels, spading forks, rakes, hoes, wheelbarrows, garden hoses, watering cans, seed starter trays, compost bins.

🚚 Pickup Rules:

Contact the volunteer garden coordinator. Volunteers often arrange porch pickup for wheelbarrows or tool bundles.

Find Community Gardens on Maps 📍 Search by ZIP Code
🧰

5. Tool Lending Libraries

Neighborhood Shared Tool Repositories
🔄 Shared Community Tooling ⚡ Power Tools Welcome

Tool libraries lend tools to community members just like public book libraries. They empower DIY repairs, urban farming, and reduce consumer waste.

✅ Best For:

Cordless drills, hedge trimmers, post-hole diggers, pruning saws, lawn aerators, rototillers, heavy wheelbarrows, specialty tools.

🔧 Bonus:

Many tool libraries have volunteer mechanics who welcome tools needing minor blade sharpening or cord repair!

Find Tool Libraries Near Me 📍 Run Safety Checklist
🏫

6. School Gardens & FFA

Youth Education & Horticulture Programs
🎓 Youth Education 📄 School District Tax Receipt

Elementary school learning gardens, middle school STEM clubs, and high school FFA / vocational landscaping programs operate on tight budgets and welcome safe tools.

✅ Best For:

Clean hand trowels, garden gloves, hand rakes, safe battery trimmers, greenhouse supplies, wheelbarrows, garden hoses.

🚚 How to Donate:

Call the school district front desk or contact the vocational agriculture instructor / biology teacher directly.

Find School Programs 📍 Create Donation Receipt
♻️

7. Building Material Reuse Centers

Nonprofit Salvage & Hardware Reuse
⚡ Heavy Equipment Accepted 📄 501(c)(3) Receipt

Architectural reuse centers (such as ReBuilding Center, Construction Junction, or Resource Central) salvage hardware, building supplies, and landscape equipment.

✅ Best For:

Heavy landscape tools, gas and electric lawn mowers, post drivers, garden fencing, paving tools, bricklayers, wheelbarrows.

🚚 Pickup Rules:

Many regional salvage centers operate box trucks for pickup of large landscaping tools and building supplies.

Find Reuse Centers on Maps 📍 Estimate Fair Value
🌿

8. Urban Park Conservancies

Public Trail & Forest Stewardship
🌲 Park Trail Restoration 📄 Non-Profit Tax Receipt

Volunteer park conservancies, watershed alliances, and community trail stewardship groups rely on donated heavy-duty tools to clear invasive plants and maintain public trails.

✅ Best For:

Heavy loppers, bypass pruners, bow saws, mattocks, Pulaski axes, brush clearing tools, heavy-duty wheelbarrows.

🚚 How to Donate:

Reach out to your local city parks foundation or attend an organized monthly volunteer park restoration workday.

Find Park Conservancies 📍 Explore Summary
🛍️

9. Animal Shelter Thrift Stores

Local Pet Rescue Funding
📍 Direct Drop-Off 📄 100% Local Charity Impact

Independent thrift shops run by local humane societies, SPCA branches, and animal rescues sell donated home and garden items to fund pet food, medical care, and adoption centers.

✅ Best For:

Garden decor, hand trowels, decorative planters, patio pots, hoses, small electric tools, garden statues, watering cans.

💖 Why Choose This:

100% of proceeds stay directly in your local community saving rescue dogs and cats.

Find Shelter Thrifts 📍 Search by ZIP
🔄

10. Buy Nothing & Freecycle

Hyper-Local Neighbor-to-Neighbor Gifting
🏡 Porch / Curbside Pickup 🤝 Zero-Driving Required

Neighborhood gift economy groups let you post a photo of your garden tools, and a neighbor will happily pick them up from your porch or driveway within hours.

✅ Best For:

Literally any item in any condition! Even lawn mowers that need a carburetor clean or shears with light surface rust.

⭐ Greatest Advantage:

No lifting, packing, or driving! Simply leave the items on your front porch for contactless pickup.

Find Buy Nothing Group → Visit Freecycle.org →

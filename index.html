When donation may not be appropriate

  • Exposed electrical wiring or serious safety defects

Garden Tool Donation Guide

Practical steps for giving garden tools and lawn equipment a useful second life.

1. Identify the item

Choose the closest category: hand tools, lawn equipment, power tools, gardening supplies or large equipment. Different organizations may have different acceptance requirements.

2. Check condition

Clean, usable and safe items are generally easier for organizations to process. Power equipment deserves extra attention because electrical, mechanical and fuel-related hazards can affect acceptance.

3. Search by ZIP or city

Use the finder above to organize your search. This page provides verified-source starting points and search workflows; it does not claim that a particular local branch accepts your item unless that branch confirms it.

4. Consider community reuse

Community gardens, schools, youth programs, tool libraries and local reuse programs can sometimes use gardening supplies directly. Contact them before transporting items.

5. Prepare lawn equipment carefully

Lawn mowers, blowers, trimmers and other equipment can have stricter rules. Confirm acceptance and follow the recipient's transport and safety instructions.

6. Keep donation documentation

If you need a receipt or acknowledgment, request it from the receiving organization. This website does not determine tax deductibility or official donation values.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where can I donate garden tools?

Possible options include reuse centers, Habitat ReStore locations, Goodwill organizations, community gardens, schools, tool libraries and nonprofits. Acceptance varies by location, so verify the exact organization's policy.

Where can I donate garden tools near me?

Use a ZIP code or city search to find local reuse centers, donation organizations, community gardens, tool libraries and related programs. Contact the exact location before visiting.

Does Goodwill accept garden tools?

Goodwill organizations set their own local donation policies. Some locations may accept tools and equipment, while others may not. Contact the local organization first.

Does Habitat ReStore accept garden tools?

ReStore donation guidelines vary by location. Some may accept usable tools or home-improvement items. Check the official local ReStore policy before bringing garden equipment.

Can I donate a lawn mower?

Some organizations accept lawn mowers, but requirements vary. Confirm that the exact location accepts your mower and ask about working condition, fuel requirements and pickup or drop-off rules.

Can I donate broken or rusty garden tools?

Light wear or surface rust may be acceptable to some organizations, but severely broken, unsafe or heavily damaged tools may be better suited to repair, reuse or recycling. Ask first.

Should I clean garden tools before donating?

Yes. Remove soil, grass and loose debris, dry the item, inspect it for damage and keep accessories together when possible.

Can donated garden tools qualify for a tax deduction?

A donation may qualify under applicable tax rules when requirements are met. Keep appropriate documentation and consult current official tax guidance or a qualified tax professional. This website does not determine deductibility.

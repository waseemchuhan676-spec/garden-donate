How We Can Help

Garden Tool Donation Finder is an independent informational resource. You can contact us regarding:

  • ✏️
    Incorrect or Outdated Information: If you notice an organization profile with outdated intake details or modified policies.
  • 🔗
    Broken Links: If an official organization link or tool button does not open properly.
  • 💡
    Feature Suggestions: Ideas for new tool categories, checklists, or educational calculators.
  • 💬
    Website Feedback: General feedback on usability, accessibility, or mobile experience.
  • ❓
    General Questions: Inquiries about using our website or tools.
📍 Looking to donate items today?
Please note that Garden Tool Donation Finder is not a donation drop-off center or pickup dispatch service. We do not collect items, maintain inventory, or schedule charity trucks. To schedule a donation or confirm whether an item is currently accepted, please contact the specific charity, thrift store, or community garden directly via the links provided on our Where to Donate page.

4. Donation Inventory & Personal Receipt Worksheet

Charities often provide only a blank receipt slip. Build your itemized inventory, calculate total estimated benchmark value, and print or save a personal record for your files.

Important Tax & Receipt Notice: Generating or printing this personal inventory record does NOT constitute an official charity tax receipt and does NOT automatically make a donation tax-deductible. Official non-cash donation receipts and acknowledgments must be issued directly by a qualified 501(c)(3) tax-exempt organization. Tax deductibility depends on applicable laws and individual taxpayer circumstances. Garden Tool Donation Finder does not provide tax advice. Keep this record together with the signed receipt issued by the receiving organization, and consult a qualified tax professional.

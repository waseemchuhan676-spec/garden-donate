Habitat ReStore

Home Improvement Thrift Nonprofit
🚚 Pickup May Be Available 📄 501(c)(3) Tax Receipt

ReStore locations sell new and gently used tools, lawn equipment, and building materials to fund affordable local home construction.

🎯 Best Exact Intent:

Donors who have working lawn mowers, power tools, or large batches of garden equipment.

✅ Accepted Items:

May accept riding mowers, push mowers, string trimmers, power tools, wheelbarrows, shovels, rakes, garden hardware. Availability and accepted items can vary by local location. Contact the specific organization before visiting.

❌ Generally Declined:

Broken tools, equipment with fuel or oil leaks, recalled models, severed electrical cords.

🚚 Pickup Policy:

Pickup may be available at some local locations. Check with the specific location for current availability. Where offered, truck pickup is typically scheduled in advance and prioritized for larger equipment or multi-item lots.

Donation Etiquette: How to Avoid Rejected Items

Charity staff inspect all items upon arrival. Follow these 3 golden rules for a seamless donation experience.

🧼

1. Clean Off Soil & Rust

Caked dirt, spider webs, and thick mud cause items to be set aside for disposal. Hose down metal blades, dry thoroughly with a rag, and apply a drop of WD-40 or vegetable oil to hinges.

⛽

2. Drain Gasoline & Oil

Charity trucks and thrift warehouses cannot transport equipment containing volatile gasoline due to municipal fire safety codes. Run tanks dry or siphon old gas into a safe container.

📦

3. Bundle Accessories Together

Tie chargers to battery tools with zip ties or tape. Keep grass catcher bags, replacement blades, and owner manuals attached to the unit so the receiving organization can use it immediately.

Frequently Asked Questions About Donating Garden Tools

Quick, verified answers to common questions about pickups, mower acceptance, tax deductions, and condition requirements.

Will Habitat ReStore pick up lawn mowers from my home?

Pickup may be available at some local locations. Many Habitat ReStores offer scheduled pickup for larger items such as riding lawn mowers or bulk equipment, but policies, fees (if any), vehicle availability, and scheduling vary by local affiliate. Check with your specific local ReStore to confirm current pickup availability.

Can you donate broken or non-working garden tools?

Traditional thrift stores usually decline broken or non-working items. However, some community tool libraries accept fixable tools for repair clinics or spare parts, and neighborhood Buy Nothing or Freecycle groups often welcome project tools for DIY restoration or metal recycling. Always describe item condition accurately.

Are garden tool donations tax deductible?

Donations to qualified 501(c)(3) non-profit organizations may be tax-deductible if you itemize deductions on your federal tax return, subject to IRS limits and regulations. Donation receipts depend on the organization, and tax deductibility depends on applicable laws and individual circumstances. Users should keep their receipts and consult a qualified tax professional for tax questions. Garden Tool Donation Finder does not provide tax or legal advice.

Should I empty gasoline from a lawn mower before donating?

Yes, draining all fuel is mandatory. Municipal fire codes and safety regulations prohibit charity transport trucks and indoor warehouses from handling equipment with volatile gasoline or flammable fuel mixtures.

Where can I donate garden tools directly to community growers?

If you prefer your tools be used directly to cultivate fresh food, reach out to local community gardens, school FFA programs, and urban agricultural collectives. Shovels, wheelbarrows, garden rakes, and hoses are consistently in high demand among local community plots.
